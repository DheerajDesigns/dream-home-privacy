Dream Home Budget Calculator

Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026 · Effective 3 November 2026

Dream Home Budget Calculator is an Android app that estimates the full cost of buying or building a home in India. This policy explains what it collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can get it deleted. It covers the app only.

The app is published on Google Play by Dheeraj ("we", "us"). For anything in this policy, including a request to delete your data, write to magicalshoots@gmail.com.

In short. You can use almost all of the app without an account, and without telling us anything. If you create one, we store your name, email, phone number and the estimates you save, so that they reach you on your next device. The app shows ads, which means Google receives your device's advertising ID. We do not sell your data, and we do not collect your location, contacts, files, photos or messages.

1. Using the app without an account

You can build estimates, use the EMI calculator, work through the document checklist and use the compass without signing in. In that mode we do not collect your name, email or any estimate you build — everything stays on your phone, and nothing is sent to us.

Advertising is the one exception: ads are shown to signed-out users too, so section 4 applies either way.

2. What we collect when you create an account

What Why
Your name, email address and phone number, given at sign-up To create and identify your account, to show who you are signed in as, and to send you the email that verifies your address or resets your password
Your password Handled entirely by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see it, and it is never stored in our own records
The plans you choose to save — plan name, property type, city, locality, size, entrance facing, the prices, rates, charges, savings and loan figures you typed, your own notes, and the computed estimate and document-checklist progress So a saved estimate can be reopened, searched, shared and exported, on this device and on the next one you sign in from
Basic usage events — which screen was opened, which action was taken, and when, recorded against your account To understand which parts of the app are actually used, so the next version improves the ones that are

Everything in this table is something you typed or something you did in the app. We do not buy data about you, we do not combine it with data from other sources, and we do not try to work out who you are beyond what you have told us.

Your estimates are financial figures, not financial accounts

A saved plan contains numbers about a property you are considering — a price, your savings, a loan you might take. We never ask for and never store a bank account number, a card number, a PAN, an Aadhaar number or any other government or financial identifier. The app does not connect to a bank, does not move money and cannot transact.

3. What is stored on your phone, and stays there

  • An optional saved password. If you tick "remember me on this device" at sign-in, your password is encrypted using a key held in the Android Keystore and stored only on that phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, it is only ever filled in for the same email address, and you can erase it at any time with Forget saved password in the Profile tab.
  • Your theme choice (light, dark or automatic).
  • Any estimate you have not saved, and the checklist progress attached to it.

Android's own backup may copy the app's settings file to your Google account, as it does for most apps. The encrypted password in it cannot be decrypted on different hardware, because the key does not leave the original phone — but your email address could be recovered from such a backup. You can turn Android backup off in your phone's system settings.

4. Advertising

The app is free and is paid for by advertising, served by Google AdMob. Ads appear as banners on several screens, as a full-screen ad when you calculate an estimate, and as an optional ad you can choose to watch to unlock the full CSV export or the year-by-year loan breakdown.

To serve and measure those ads, Google receives your device's advertising ID along with technical information such as your device model, operating system version, coarse location inferred from your IP address, and whether you interacted with an ad. We do not receive your advertising ID, and we cannot tie it to your account.

How Google uses that data is governed by its own policies, not ours. See Google's advertising privacy information and AdMob's partner disclosure.

Your choice about personalised ads

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before it loads any ad, and you can change that answer at any time from Ad privacy options in the Profile tab.

Wherever you are, you can limit ad tracking on your phone: Settings → Privacy → Ads lets you delete or reset your advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads. The app keeps working either way; the ads are simply less relevant.

5. Who else sees your data

We use two Google services, and no other third party:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores your account and your saved plans on our behalf, under Firebase's privacy and security terms.
  • Google AdMob serves the ads, as described above.

We do not sell your personal data, we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not give it to data brokers, advertisers or anyone else for their own purposes. We would disclose it only if we were legally required to — for example by a valid court order — or where it is necessary to investigate abuse of the service.

Where it is stored

Firebase stores this data on Google's servers, which may be located outside India, including in the United States. Google applies its own safeguards for such transfers, including the European Commission's standard contractual clauses where they apply.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account and saved plans: for as long as your account exists.
  • A plan you delete in the app: removed from our database when you delete it.
  • Usage events: kept with your account and deleted with it.
  • When you delete your account in the app: removed immediately. If you ask us to delete it by email instead, within 30 days.

Signing out does not delete anything — it only ends the session on that phone, so your plans are still there when you sign back in.

7. Deleting your data

You can do all of this yourself, in the app, and you do not need a reason:

  1. Delete individual plans — the ⋮ menu on any card in the Plans tab. This takes effect immediately.
  2. Delete your whole account — Profile → Delete my account. You will be asked to confirm and to enter your password, and then your account, every saved plan and your whole usage record are deleted straight away. This cannot be undone, and we cannot recover any of it for you afterwards.

If you cannot reach the app — you have changed phones, or you have forgotten your password — email magicalshoots@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete the account for you within 30 days.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, to receive a copy in a portable form, and to withdraw a consent you have given. Users in India have these rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023; users in the EEA and the UK have them under the GDPR and UK GDPR.

To exercise any of them, write to magicalshoots@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EEA or the UK and are unhappy with our response, you may complain to your national data protection authority.

Where we need a legal basis under the GDPR: we process your account details and saved plans to perform the contract you enter into by creating an account; we rely on your consent for personalised advertising; and we rely on our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app for the basic usage events, and in keeping the service secure.

9. Security

Your account is protected by Firebase Authentication, and our database rules allow each signed-in user to read and write only their own documents — no user can reach another user's plans. Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS, and data at rest is encrypted by Google. Any password you choose to save on your phone is encrypted with a hardware-backed key that never leaves it.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we ever become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

10. Children

The app is meant for adults making property decisions and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us their data, write to us and we will delete it.

11. Permissions the app asks for

The app requests internet access only. The Google services it includes also declare permissions for the advertising ID, network state and keeping the device awake while an ad plays.

The compass on the Home screen reads your phone's orientation sensor, which needs no permission and reveals nothing about where you are. It is not location: we do not request, collect or store your GPS position. The city and locality on a plan are there because you typed them.

12. The estimates themselves

Not a privacy matter, but worth being as clear about: this app is a planning and estimating tool, in the same category as an EMI calculator. Its figures are estimates based on rates you can see and change. It is not financial, tax or legal advice, and it is not a quote. Confirm stamp duty and guideline values with your local registration office, and taxes with a qualified adviser, before you rely on a number.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles your data, we will update this page and change the date at the top. For a change that materially affects you, we will also say so in the app. The date above is always the authoritative one.
